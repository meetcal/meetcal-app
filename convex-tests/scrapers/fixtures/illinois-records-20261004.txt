Revised: 09/13/2026
Records were converted from the old records using the same criteria USAW used for their records:
1. Standards were drawn from pre-existing bodyweight divisions.
2. If no one holds a record, and what is still there is astandard, we applied a 5kg reduction to
thatstandard.Standards cannot be lowered to less than 15kg for women and 20kg for men.
3. If the bodyweight divisions remain the same, the former record holder will KEEP its record.
4. If a pre-existing bodyweight division "became heavier", a standard will be further increased by that
difference (1kg more to the standard if going from 36kg to 37kg bodyweight, for example)
5. If a pre-existing bodyweight division "became lighter", then the standard will be further decreased by
that difference plus one. So, for a bodyweight that was 63kg and then became 61kg, the standards will be
a. If the TOTAL was previously astandard, the newstandard should be the sum of thestandard in the
snatch and the record in the clean and jerk. We only applied this rule if both the snatch and clean and jerk
6. A record will be ZERO if no one has ever attempted that weight.
Additional information on how USAW determined their new standards can be found on the
USAW Records Page
